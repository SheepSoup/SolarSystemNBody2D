just a simple simulation of the orbits in the solar system squashed into a 2d plane  
  
This simulation:  
-uses the gravity equation and calculates position, velocity and acceleration with vectors in two dimensions  
-calculates gravity between every body  
-contains Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto  
-is based off of positions at earths last perihelion(jan 3 2026 i believe?)  
-contains very inaccurate starting vectors for the velocity of planets  
  
This simulation DOES NOT:  
-contain moons... yet  
-do calculations in 3 dimensions... yet
